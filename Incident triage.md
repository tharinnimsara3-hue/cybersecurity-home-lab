# Incident Triage

## What is Incident Triage?

Incident triage is the process of reviewing security alerts to determine whether they are real threats or false positives.

## Incident Triage Process

1. Receive the alert
2. Collect evidence
3. Analyze logs
4. Determine severity
5. Decide whether the event is malicious
6. Escalate if necessary
7. Document the findings

## Severity Levels

Critical
High
Medium
Low

## What I Learned

Incident triage helps SOC analysts prioritize security incidents and respond efficiently.
