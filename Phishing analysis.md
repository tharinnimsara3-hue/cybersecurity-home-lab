# Phishing Analysis

## What is Phishing?

Phishing is a cyber attack where attackers trick users into revealing sensitive information such as passwords, banking details, or personal information.

## Common Signs of Phishing

- Suspicious sender address
- Urgent language
- Unexpected attachments
- Fake login pages
- Misspelled URLs
- Requests for passwords or payment

## How to Analyze a Phishing Email

1. Check the sender's email address.
2. Inspect links before clicking.
3. Look for spelling and grammar mistakes.
4. Verify attachments before opening.
5. Confirm the email with the organization if unsure.

## Prevention

- User awareness training
- Multi-factor authentication (MFA)
- Email filtering
- Verify suspicious emails

## What I Learned

Phishing is one of the most common cyber attacks, and SOC analysts investigate suspicious emails to protect users and organizations.
