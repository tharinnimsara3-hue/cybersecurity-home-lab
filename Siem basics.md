# SIEM Basics

## What is SIEM?

SIEM stands for Security Information and Event Management.

A SIEM collects logs from multiple devices, stores them in one place, and helps security analysts detect suspicious activities.

## Why is SIEM important?

- Centralizes logs
- Detects attacks
- Generates alerts
- Helps investigate incidents
- Supports compliance and reporting

## Examples of SIEM Tools

- Splunk
- Wazuh
- Elastic (ELK Stack)
- Microsoft Sentinel
- IBM QRadar

## What I Learned

A SIEM helps security teams monitor networks and systems by collecting and analyzing logs from different sources.
