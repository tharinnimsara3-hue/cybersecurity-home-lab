# Vulnerability Risk Rating

## Risk Formula
Risk = Likelihood × Impact

## Severity Levels

### Critical
Very serious vulnerabilities requiring immediate remediation.

### High
Major vulnerabilities that should be fixed quickly.

### Medium
Moderate vulnerabilities with limited impact.

### Low
Minor vulnerabilities that should still be addressed.

## Factors Affecting Risk

### Likelihood
- Ease of exploitation
- Required attacker skill
- Availability of exploit tools

### Impact
- Confidentiality loss
- Integrity loss
- Availability loss

## What I Learned
Risk ratings help organizations prioritize remediation efforts effectively.
