# Mini Incident Report

## Incident Title

Multiple Failed Login Attempts Detected

---

## Date

(Add today's date.)

---

## Incident Summary

Twenty-five failed login attempts were detected against a user account within ten minutes. The activity was identified during routine security monitoring.

---

## Severity

Medium

---

## Affected System

- Windows Workstation

---

## Indicators Observed

- Multiple authentication failures.
- Unusual login behavior.
- Potential password guessing activity.

---

## Investigation Performed

The following actions were taken:

- Reviewed Windows Security Logs.
- Examined failed login events.
- Checked for successful login attempts after the failures.
- Investigated unusual account activity.

---

## Containment Actions

- Increased monitoring of the affected account.
- Recommended resetting the account password.
- Recommended enabling Multi-Factor Authentication.

---

## Recovery Actions

- Verified that no unauthorized access occurred.
- Restored normal account access.
- Continued monitoring for suspicious behavior.

---

## Lessons Learned

Monitoring failed login attempts helps identify suspicious activities before they become serious security incidents.

---

## What I Learned

This exercise improved my understanding of incident response procedures, log analysis, and professional security reporting.
